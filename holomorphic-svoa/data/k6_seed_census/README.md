# Data of the k = 6 seed census (details.html §C.2, §G.3)

The 7,112 k = 6 seed codes of the two censuses, the inputs of the scripts in `../../code/census/`, and the per-seed
records and summaries of our runs (20 October 2026). `FILES.json` lists every file with its workspace source and
SHA-256; `tools/package_k6_census.py` (workspace) wrote this folder.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `hp_census_hits.jsonl` | Hadamard–Paley census (details.html §C.1, §C.2): one line per seed cell (Tr JM = 0, \|Tr JM³\| = 24), 1,081 lines, 1,078 distinct cells (three lines repeat a cell). `blocks`, `parities`: the signed partition of the 12 points; `R`: boundary coordinates (`r` = \|R\|, 2–6); `rep`, `orbit_size`: the block configuration's orbit representative; `I1`, `I2`, `I3` = Tr JM, Tr JM², Tr JM³; `nmax` = number of full-support codewords N; `IL`, `nz_eigs`: traces Tr JM^L (L = 1..6) and number of nonzero eigenvalues. Lines with r = 2–5 come from the exhaustive phase (204,490 cells), lines with r = 6 from the six-spin phase, which was stopped after 17 of the 130 block configurations (15,708 of 120,120 cells) |
| `refl_census_hits.jsonl` | reflection census: cells Q = R_v R_w (two reflections on the Paley base, v an orbit representative, w random of weight 4–8), every R with 2 ≤ \|R\| ≤ 5; 6,034 seed cells, all distinct, all from this random phase (the exhaustive one-reflection phase, 437,294 cells, gives none). `left`: the vectors v, w; `key`, `phase`, `support`: bookkeeping of the search; other fields as above |
| `fc_seed_census.jsonl.gz` | output of `code/census/fc_seed_census.py`, one record per seed: `N`, `F_table` (= F), `I` (index), `I1`; `F_direct_even_odd` (F recomputed from the generator, split by parity), `fixed_dim`, `Cbar_self_dual`; `n_total` = n₊ + n₋ = (N + 8F)/6, `n_plus_minus`; `rowspace_sha256`; `key` (the seed), `census` (hp or refl), `class_key` (canonical form of its symmetry class) |
| `fc_seed_level1_part1of2.jsonl.gz`, `fc_seed_level1_part2of2.jsonl.gz` | output of `code/census/fc_seed_level1.py` (two parts, disjoint): `N`, `A69` (codewords with exactly three zeros), `A6` (weight 6), `dim_T1` = A6/6, `Cbar_enum` (weight enumerator of the contracted g²-fixed code), `X_twisted`, `D1` (states at the first excited Ramond level, L₀ = 4), `level1_even_odd`, `key` |
| `fc_seed_level2_part1of2.jsonl.gz`, `fc_seed_level2_part2of2.jsonl.gz` | output of `code/census/fc_seed_level2.py`: `A` (A₆, A₉, A₁₂, A₁₅), `A3_Cbar`, `T1`, `T32`, `T2`, `T52` (dim T_h for h ≤ 5/2), `a` (a₀, …, a₆, the polynomial below), `D1_pred` = −4096 a₁ (equal to `D1` of level 1 for every seed), the derived NS dimensions `T3`, `T72`, `T4` and Ramond levels `D2`, `D3`, `D4`, `key` |
| `k6_seed_polynomials.csv` | **the NS character of every seed theory as a polynomial in t** = (ϑ₃/η)¹²: Z_T = Tr_NS q^(L₀−3) = a₆t⁶ + … + a₁t + a₀ (no (−1)^F inserted); the same coefficients give Σ (−1)^j a_j (ϑ₄/η)^(12j) with (−1)^F and the Ramond character Σ (−1)^j a_j (ϑ₂/η)^(12j). A comment header explains the columns: `census`, `key`, `rowspace_sha256`, `class_id` (symmetry class), `sign_I` (sign of the Witten index), `a6`, …, `a0`. Written by `code/census/fc_seed_polynomials.py`; 7,112 lines, 2,961 distinct polynomials |
| `fc_seed_census_summary.json`, `fc_seed_level1_summary.json`, `fc_seed_level2_summary.json`, `k6_seed_polynomials_summary.json` | the summaries the scripts write (`--summarize`, or together with the CSV); regenerated byte for byte from the records above, except the `file` field of the last (the path the CSV was written to) |
| `k6_seed_codes.json` | output of `code/census/count_k6_seed_codes.py`: 7,112 distinct codes (rowspace hashes), all self-dual, none in both censuses; the paper's seed (certificate k06_claude_canonical) is one of them |

The two `*_hits.jsonl` files are copies of the census workers' hit files with the bulky fields removed (the 9×9 transfer
table `T`, its numerical eigenvalues `eigs` and the constant flag `hit`); every line is kept, in its original order, so
the scripts enumerate the seeds in the same order as in our runs. The `.gz` files are plain gzip (mtime 0); to rerun
`--summarize` on our records, unpack them into `../../code/out/census/` first.
