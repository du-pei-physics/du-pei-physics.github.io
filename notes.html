Notes by AI

Agentic AI is revolutionizing our field. Our community is still working out how to adapt, with much uncertainty in the air. I believe that greater transparency is the best way to move our field forward. In particular, I think it is now equally important to share both successes and failures, so that efforts across the field can be better coordinated.

Below I share some notes written by AI that may be of interest. Some are notes I have found educational, exploring questions I have long had but have not had the energy or expertise to investigate myself. Others document my unsuccessful attempts, with AI, to answer research questions, so that other people—and perhaps more importantly, agents—can learn from them. I also include some results I like but feel too embarrassed to write up as a paper, since my own involvement was minimal.

Companions of papers

Companions to the preprint Construction of holomorphic SCFTs with minimal index:

  • Holomorphic SCFTs with minimal index

    Constructions of holomorphic N = 1 SCFTs with central charge 12k attaining the TMF-predicted minimal nonzero absolute Witten index. Includes code constructions, orbifold arguments, explicit certificates and verification code.

  • The index of self-dual ternary codes

    Presents a proof of a conjecture by Gaiotto and Johnson-Freyd: the index of every self-dual ternary code of positive length is divisible by 24, and codes of index 24 exist at every length 12k. Includes an explicit Golay-chain construction and a machine-checkable certificate.
